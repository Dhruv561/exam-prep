The usual question order

2025 S2, the 2026 practice and 2025 S1 all run in syllabus order, about 10 marks per question.

  1. Q1–Q2T1–T2
    Futures. A margin account with two positions in the same contract, or a hedge: cross-hedge h* from a correlation matrix, or a beta change with index futures. Parts: long or short, which maturity, number of contracts, full hedge plan, basis risk.
  2. Q2–Q3T3
    Forward pricing. Convenience yield, or index or FX forward arbitrage with a cash-flow table.
  3. Q3–Q4case / T4
    Conceptual article or case question (about 7 marks), or options basics: time value of an American put, effect of a dividend announcement.
  4. Q5T5
    Binomial, 10–12 marks. A 2–3 step put (European or American), u = eσ√Δt. Then the delta hedge, whether Δ0 still hedges at t = 1, an arbitrage when the quoted price is off, and the "risk preferences play no role" statement.
  5. Q6T7–T8
    Lognormal and BSM. P(call exercised), or a confidence interval and E/Var of ST. Theory on binomial vs BSM (assumption 1, convergence 1, link 2). Sometimes dividend, FX or index BSM, or implied q from parity.
  6. Q7T9
    Delta hedging. Portfolio or index-option delta hedge, then rebalancing after a price move. Futures vs forward delta, with the intuition.
  7. Q8T10
    Rates and swaps, 8–10 marks. Swap design with the bank's cut, a percentage split of the gains and a diagram (4–6 marks). Zero rates by bootstrapping, par yield, or the two-bond trick.

Patterns to know

Every recent paper

  • A binomial put with a delta hedge
  • A delta-hedge-then-rebalance question
  • A futures hedge (cross-hedge or beta)
  • Swap design plus a zero-rate calculation (since 2025)
  • Lognormal probability or interval (since 2025)

Reuse and drift

  • The 2025 S2 margin question reuses the 2022 numbers exactly.
  • The 2025 S1 binomial, lognormal and swap questions reappear in the 2026 practice.
  • Gamma/vega-neutral questions appear only in 2022 and the Sample. The 2026 slides only define those Greeks.
  • Futures-option (Black) pricing appears once, in 2025 S1 (6 marks).
