# Derivative Securities (FNCE30007) Resources

## Knowledge

- Lecture slides: `lectures/Lecture N_*.pdf` (Dr Xiaoting Wei, UniMelb, Sem 2 2026)
  Defines the course's notation and scope, so it's the top authority. Use for: definitions, worked examples, which twists are examinable. Lecture 1 = futures intro (reading: Hull Ch. 1–2).
- Textbook: John C. Hull, *Options, Futures and Other Derivatives* (Pearson). The course readings point to its chapters.
  Use for: fuller explanations and end-of-chapter problems (Ch. 2 margins and futures markets; Ch. 3 hedging (basis risk, cross hedging, MVHR, stock index futures, rolling); Ch. 4 interest rates (continuous compounding); Ch. 5 forward and futures prices (income, yield, currencies, commodities, convenience yield, valuing forwards); Ch. 10+ options).
- Tutorial sheets + solutions: `tutes/Tutorial N(.pdf| solutions.pdf)`
  Official practice. T1 = futures/margins, T2 = hedging, T3 = pricing, T4 = options intro, T5–6 = binomial, T7 = BSM, T8 = dividends/indices, T9 = delta hedging.
- `Derivative Securities Notes.pdf` (student summary, 2019)
  Use for: quick second explanations. Older syllabus, so check anything against the slides.
- [CME Group Education: "Margin: Know What's Needed"](https://www.cmegroup.com/education/courses/introduction-to-futures/margin-know-what-is-needed.html)
  The exchange's own explanation of initial and maintenance margin and margin calls (a call restores the account to the initial level). Use for: margin mechanics.
- [ASX 24 contract specifications (PDF)](https://www.asx.com.au/content/dam/asx/participants/derivatives-market/ird/asx24-contract-specifications.pdf)
  Official specs; the SPI 200 is A$25 per index point and cash-settled. Use for: checking contract multipliers in lecture examples.
- [Investor.gov glossary: futures contract (US SEC)](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/glossary/futures-contract)
  A plain-language regulator definition. Use for: a first-contact refresher.

- Past exams: `exams/`, `MST/`. **Reserved for the user to sit timed: never use their questions in lessons. Mine them for patterns only.**
