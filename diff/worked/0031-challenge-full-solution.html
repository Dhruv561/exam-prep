The struck string, written out as an exam answer

Everything inside the red rule is what goes on your paper. The grey notes explain the reasoning, and you don't write them. Printing this page hides the notes.

A taut string of length $1$, wave speed $c=3$, fixed at both ends. At $t=0$: displacement $\frac1{20}\sin(2\pi x)$; the middle third $\frac13<x<\frac23$ is struck and given velocity $v_0>0$, the rest has zero velocity.

(a) PDE, BCs, ICs. [2] (b) Separate: $X''-\lambda X=0$, $\ddot T-9\lambda T=0$. [2] (c) Three cases, eigenpairs. [4] (d) General solution, normal frequencies. [2] (e) Apply ICs. [5] (f) Absent harmonics, period. [2] (g) Steady state? Kinks? [1]

(a) · The IBVP

$$u_{tt}=9u_{xx},\qquad 0<x<1,\ t>0,$$ $$u(0,t)=u(1,t)=0\quad(t>0),$$ $$u(x,0)=\tfrac1{20}\sin(2\pi x),\qquad u_t(x,0)=g(x)=\begin{cases}v_0,&\frac13<x<\frac23\\0,&\text{otherwise}\end{cases}\quad(0<x<1).$$

$c^2=9$, not $3$. "Fixed at both ends" → Dirichlet. "Struck…velocity" → the $u_t$ IC.

(b) · Separation

Substitute $u=X(x)T(t)$: $X\ddot T=9X''T$. Divide by $9XT$: $$\frac{\ddot T}{9T}=\frac{X''}{X}.$$ The left side depends only on $t$, the right only on $x$, so both equal a constant $\lambda$: $$X''-\lambda X=0,\qquad \ddot T-9\lambda T=0.$$ BCs: $u(0,t)=X(0)T(t)=0$ for all $t>0$; $T\not\equiv0$ (else $u\equiv0$), so $X(0)=0$. Likewise $X(1)=0$.

(c) · Three cases

$\lambda=\mu^2>0$: $X=A\cosh\mu x+B\sinh\mu x$. $X(0)=A=0$; $X(1)=B\sinh\mu=0$ with $\sinh\mu\ne0$ ⇒ $B=0$. Trivial.

$\lambda=0$: $X=A+Bx$. $X(0)=A=0$, $X(1)=B=0$. Trivial.

$\lambda=-k^2<0$: $X=A\cos kx+B\sin kx$. $X(0)=A=0$; $X(1)=B\sin k=0$, non-trivial needs $\sin k=0$, $k=n\pi$.

$$\lambda_n=-n^2\pi^2,\qquad X_n(x)=\sin(n\pi x),\qquad n=1,2,3,\dots$$

$n=0$ gives $X\equiv0$ and negative $n$ repeat the same functions, so $n\ge1$. One mark per case plus one for the eigenpairs is typical.

(d) · General solution

$\ddot T_n-9\lambda_nT_n=\ddot T_n+9n^2\pi^2T_n=0$ ⇒ $T_n=a_n\cos\omega_nt+b_n\sin\omega_nt$, $\;\omega_n=3n\pi$. $$u(x,t)=\sum_{n=1}^\infty\big(a_n\cos(3n\pi t)+b_n\sin(3n\pi t)\big)\sin(n\pi x).$$ Normal frequencies $\omega_n=ck_n=3n\pi$, $n=1,2,\dots$

(e) · Initial conditions

Displacement. $u(x,0)=\sum a_n\sin(n\pi x)=\frac1{20}\sin(2\pi x)$. Comparing coefficients: $a_2=\frac1{20}$, $a_n=0$ for $n\ne2$.

Velocity. $u_t(x,0)=\sum \omega_nb_n\sin(n\pi x)=g(x)$, a sine series on $(0,1)$, so $$3n\pi\,b_n=2\int_0^1g(x)\sin(n\pi x)\,dx=2v_0\int_{1/3}^{2/3}\sin(n\pi x)\,dx=\frac{2v_0}{n\pi}\Big[\cos\frac{n\pi}3-\cos\frac{2n\pi}3\Big].$$ $$\boxed{b_n=\frac{2v_0}{3n^2\pi^2}\Big(\cos\frac{n\pi}3-\cos\frac{2n\pi}3\Big)}$$ $$u(x,t)=\frac1{20}\cos(6\pi t)\sin(2\pi x)+\frac{2v_0}{3\pi^2}\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{\cos\frac{n\pi}3-\cos\frac{2n\pi}3}{n^2}\,\sin(3n\pi t)\sin(n\pi x).$$

Dividing by $\omega_n=3n\pi$ is the mark most often dropped. Equivalent form via sum-to-product: $\cos\frac{n\pi}3-\cos\frac{2n\pi}3=2\sin\frac{n\pi}2\sin\frac{n\pi}6$. First values of $\frac{3\pi^2}{v_0}b_n$: $2,0,-\frac49,0,\frac2{25},0,\frac2{49},\dots$ (checked numerically: the velocity series returns $\approx v_0$ inside $(\frac13,\frac23)$ and $\approx0$ outside).

(f) · Absent harmonics, period

Mode $n$ is absent iff $a_n=b_n=0$. For even $n$: $\cos\frac{n\pi}3=\cos\frac{2n\pi}3$ ($n=2m$ ⇒ $\cos\frac{4m\pi}3=\cos\big(2m\pi-\frac{2m\pi}3\big)=\cos\frac{2m\pi}3$), so $b_n=0$. For odd $n$, $\cos\frac{n\pi}3-\cos\frac{2n\pi}3=2\sin\frac{n\pi}2\sin\frac{n\pi}6\ne0$.
$a_2\ne0$, so $n=2$ is present. Absent: $n=4,6,8,\dots$ (even $n\ge4$).

Every term has period dividing $\frac{2\pi}{\omega_1}=\frac{2\pi}{3\pi}=\frac23$. Period $\frac23$ $\big(=\frac{2L}c\big)$.

Why even $n$ vanish from the strike: the strike is symmetric about $x=\frac12$, and even modes are antisymmetric about $x=\frac12$. Odd $n$: $\sin\frac{n\pi}6\ne0$ because $6\nmid n$. Don't forget the displacement brings back $n=2$; "all even $n$ absent" loses the mark.

(g) · Long time

No: the modes $\cos(3n\pi t)$, $\sin(3n\pi t)$ do not decay, so there is no steady state. The kinks at $x=\frac13,\frac23$ are not smoothed; they propagate along the string at speed $3$, reflect at the fixed ends, and the motion repeats with period $\frac23$.
