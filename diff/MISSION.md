# Mission: Differential Equations (MAST20030)

## Why
Do well in the MAST20030 Differential Equations exam on **5 November 2026**, starting from scratch on 12 October 2026 (about 3.5 weeks).

## Success looks like
- Can solve exam-level problems from all six topics without notes, in exam time
- Can recognise which method a problem calls for (integrating factor, reduction of order, eigenvalues, Laplace, Fourier, separation of variables, …)
- Handles the "harder than the lecture notes" twists: non-standard forms, messy integrals, multi-step IVPs, conceptual questions (Wronskian, solution-space dimension, stability)

## Constraints
- Exam questions are harder than the lecture-note exercises: practise above that level
- Six topics in ~3.5 weeks: roughly 4 days per topic, with time left for revision and past papers
- Course materials: `lectures/` (Topic 1 = files 01–06), `problem-booklet/`, `tutes/`
- Past exams in `exams/` are reserved for the user to sit as practice exams. Lessons adapt their patterns but never their questions
- Exam format (2024): 3 h, no calculator, no notes sheet; a formulae sheet covers Laplace/Fourier only, so Topic 1 methods must be memorised

## Out of scope
- Material not in the MAST20030 syllabus (e.g. existence proofs, path-ordered exponentials beyond a mention)
