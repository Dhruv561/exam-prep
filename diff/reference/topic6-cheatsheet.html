Topic 6: Fourier transforms

Lessons 34–38, lectures 28–30. [S] = on the formula sheet (definition, inverse, derivative rule, $x^n$ rule, convolution theorem). memorise = not on the sheet: the table, shifts, midpoint rule, heat kernel. Booklet writes $\omega$ for $k$.

Convention [S]

$$\hat f(k)=\mathcal F\{f\}=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(x)e^{-ikx}dx$$ $$f(x)=\mathcal F^{-1}\{\hat f\}=\frac1{2\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\hat f(k)e^{ikx}dk$$

$\delta_a(x)=\delta(x-a)$, $H_a(x)=H(x-a)$ (step at $a$), $\operatorname{sinc}y=\frac{\sin y}y$, $\operatorname{sinc}0=1$. Exists if $f$ absolutely integrable + piecewise continuous.

Jumps memorise: $\mathcal F^{-1}\mathcal F f=\tfrac12\big(f(x^-)+f(x^+)\big)$ at a jump, $f(x)$ elsewhere. A value at one point (e.g. $f(0)=\pi/2$) doesn't change $\hat f$.

Duality memorise: $\mathcal F\{\hat f(x)\}=2\pi f(-k)$.

From the definition (exam favourites):

  • $e^{-ax}H(x)\leftrightarrow\frac1{a+ik}$; $\;e^{ax}H(-x)\leftrightarrow\frac1{a-ik}$ ($a\gt0$).
  • $H_a-H_b$ ($a\lt b$, 1 on $(a,b)$) $\leftrightarrow\frac{e^{-iak}-e^{-ibk}}{ik}$; value at $k=0$ is $b-a$. Step functions $\sum c_jH_{a_j}$: sum of these.
  • Even ext: $\hat f=2\int_0^\infty f\cos kx\,dx$ (real, even). Odd ext: $\hat f=-2i\int_0^\infty f\sin kx\,dx$ (imaginary, odd).

Shift proofs: substitute $u=x-y$ (one line); $e^{iqx}e^{-ikx}=e^{-i(k-q)x}$. Combined: $xf'$ ↦ $i\frac d{dk}(ik\hat f)=-\hat f-k\hat f'$.

Proofs ("show that")

Derivative (by parts): $\int f'e^{-ikx}dx=\big[fe^{-ikx}\big]_{-\infty}^{\infty}+ik\int fe^{-ikx}dx=ik\hat f$; boundary term $=0$ since $f\to0$ at $\pm\infty$ (say so).

Times $x$: $xe^{-ikx}=i\frac{\partial}{\partial k}e^{-ikx}$, pull $\frac d{dk}$ outside.

Convolution theorem (4 lines): $$\mathcal F\{f*g\}=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\!\Big(\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(x-\xi)g(\xi)\,d\xi\Big)e^{-ikx}dx$$ $$=\int g(\xi)e^{-ik\xi}\Big(\int f(x-\xi)e^{-ik(x-\xi)}dx\Big)d\xi\ \text{(swap)}$$ $$=\int g(\xi)e^{-ik\xi}\Big(\int f(u)e^{-iku}du\Big)d\xi\quad (u=x-\xi)$$ $$=\hat f(k)\,\hat g(k).$$

Use it: $\mathcal F\{\frac1{(1+x^2)^2}\}=\frac1{2\pi}\pi^2\!\int e^{-|k-q|}e^{-|q|}dq=\frac\pi2(1+|k|)e^{-|k|}$ (split $q\lt0$, $0\lt q\lt k$, $q\gt k$ for $k\gt0$; even in $k$). Box∗box: $(2a-|x|)_+\leftrightarrow4a^2\operatorname{sinc}^2ak$. $\frac1{x^2+4}*\frac1{x^2+9}=\frac{5\pi}6\frac1{x^2+25}$ (L29 Ex 6.5).

Inverses by recognition

  • $\cos ak=\frac12(e^{iak}+e^{-iak})\ \Rightarrow\ \mathcal F^{-1}=\frac12\big[\delta(x+a)+\delta(x-a)\big]$
  • $\sin ak=\frac1{2i}(e^{iak}-e^{-iak})\ \Rightarrow\ \mathcal F^{-1}=\frac i2\big[\delta(x-a)-\delta(x+a)\big]$
  • $e^{-iak}\hat g(k)\Rightarrow g(x-a)$; $\hat g(k-q)\Rightarrow e^{iqx}g(x)$. E.g. $\frac{2e^{-2ik}}{1+k^2}\Rightarrow e^{-|x-2|}$.
  • Derivatives of table entries: $\frac{d\hat g}{dk}\Rightarrow -ix\,g$. E.g. $\frac{x}{x^2+a^2}\leftrightarrow-i\pi\operatorname{sgn}(k)e^{-a|k|}$.

Real integrals from a pair

$\int f\,dx=\hat f(0)$; $\ f(0)=\frac1{2\pi}\int\hat f\,dk$; $\ \int f(x)\cos bx\,dx=\frac12[\hat f(b)+\hat f(-b)]$ ($=\hat f(b)$ if $f$ even).

$\int e^{-ax^2}\cos bx\,dx=\sqrt{\frac\pi a}e^{-b^2/4a}$; $\int\frac{\cos bx}{x^2+a^2}dx=\frac\pi ae^{-a|b|}$; $\int\frac{\sin ax}{x}dx=\pi$ ($a\gt0$, sinc pair at $k=0$).

ODE recipe (L29–30)

Constant coeff $P(D)y=f$: $P(ik)\hat y=\hat f\Rightarrow\hat y=\hat f/P(ik)$; partial fractions in $k$ → table, or write as convolution, or leave $y=\frac1{2\pi}\int\frac{\hat f}{P(ik)}e^{ikx}dk$.

$y''-a^2y=f\ \Rightarrow\ \hat y=-\frac{\hat f}{a^2+k^2}\ \Rightarrow\ y=-\frac1{2a}e^{-a|x|}*f$. $\ f=\delta$: $y=-\frac1{2a}e^{-a|x|}$.

"Subject to its FT existing" = keep only the decaying solution; $e^{\pm ax}$ homogeneous pieces have no FT and vanish.

Variable coeff (coeffs linear in $x$):

  1. Transform: $\mathcal F\{xy\}=i\hat y'$, $\mathcal F\{xy^{(n)}\}=i\frac{d}{dk}[(ik)^n\hat y]$
  2. Simplify to 1st-order linear: $\hat y'=g(k)\hat y$
  3. Integrating factor / separate: $\hat y=Ae^{\int g\,dk}$
  4. Invert: $y=\frac A{2\pi}\int e^{\int g\,dk}e^{ikx}dk$ (leave as integral)
  5. Say: "most general solution with a FT" (one constant; the other solution, e.g. Bi, has none)

Airy $y''-xy=0$: $-k^2\hat y-i\hat y'=0\Rightarrow\hat y=Ae^{ik^3/3}$. $\ y'+xy=0$: $\hat y'=-k\hat y\Rightarrow\hat y=Ae^{-k^2/2}$ (check: $y\propto e^{-x^2/2}$).

PDE recipe on $\mathbb R$ (L30)

FT in $x$: $u_x\mapsto ik\hat u$, $u_{xx}\mapsto-k^2\hat u$, $t$ (or $y$) is a parameter ⇒ ODE in $t$ with "constants" $A(k)$. IC: $\hat u(k,0)=\hat f(k)$.

  • Heat $u_t=\alpha u_{xx}$: $\hat u=\hat f(k)e^{-\alpha k^2t}$, $u=\frac1{2\pi}\int\hat fe^{-\alpha k^2t}e^{ikx}dk=G_t*f$.
    memorise kernel ($f=\delta$): $G_t(x)=\frac1{2\sqrt{\pi\alpha t}}e^{-x^2/4\alpha t}$. $f=e^{-x^2}$: $u=\frac1{\sqrt{1+4\alpha t}}e^{-x^2/(1+4\alpha t)}$.
  • Wave $u_{tt}=c^2u_{xx}$, $u=f$, $u_t=g$: IVP $\hat u_{tt}+c^2k^2\hat u=0$, $\hat u(k,0)=\hat f$, $\hat u_t(k,0)=\hat g$ ⇒ $\hat u=\hat f\cos ckt+\hat g\frac{\sin ckt}{ck}$ ⇒ d'Alembert $u=\frac12[f(x-ct)+f(x+ct)]+\frac1{2c}\int_{x-ct}^{x+ct}g$ (cos → two shifts).
  • Advection $u_t=cu_x$: $\hat u=\hat fe^{ickt}$ ⇒ $u=f(x+ct)$ (shift by $-ct$); $f=\delta$: $\delta(x+ct)$.
  • Laplace strip $0\lt y\lt1$, $u(x,0)=0$, $u(x,1)=g$: $\hat u_{yy}=k^2\hat u$ ⇒ $A\cosh ky+B\sinh ky$ ⇒ $\hat u=\hat g\frac{\sinh ky}{\sinh k}$. $g$ even ⇒ $\hat g$ real even ⇒ $\sin kx$ part odd, integrates to 0: $u=\frac1\pi\int_0^\infty\hat g\frac{\sinh ky}{\sinh k}\cos kx\,dk$.

Exam traps

  • $\mathcal F\{xf\}=+i\hat f'$ (not $-i$). Derivative: $(ik)^n$, so $f''\mapsto-k^2\hat f$.
  • $\frac1{2\pi}$ on the inverse only; $e^{iax}\leftrightarrow2\pi\delta_a$ carries the $2\pi$.
  • Shift signs: $f(x-y)\mapsto e^{-iky}$; $e^{iqx}f\mapsto\hat f(k-q)$.
  • FT only finds solutions that have a FT: $e^{\pm x}$, Bi, growing solutions are lost.
  • $H_a=H(x-a)$ is a step at $a$; $H_{-a}-H_a$ is the box on $(-a,a)$, not $H_a-H_{-a}$.
  • Value at a jump: midpoint (booklet Q1 defines $f(0)=\pi/2$ to match).
  • Check: real even $f$ ⇒ $\hat f$ real even; real odd ⇒ $\hat f$ imaginary odd; $\hat f(0)=\int f$.
  • Other books: $\frac1{\sqrt{2\pi}}$ or $e^{+ikx}$. Convert before copying a table.
