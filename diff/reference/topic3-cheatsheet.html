Resonance: $\mathcal L^{-1}\!\left\{\frac1{(s^2+\omega^2)^2}\right\}=\frac{\sin\omega t-\omega t\cos\omega t}{2\omega^3}$. Also $\mathcal L\{t^nf\}=(-1)^nF^{(n)}$.

Trig first: $\cos^2t-\sin^2t=\cos2t$, $\sin^2t=\tfrac12(1-\cos2t)$, $\sin t\cos t=\tfrac12\sin2t$. Then s-shift for any $e^{at}$ factor.

Inverse transforms

  • Distinct roots: cover-up. Repeated $(s-a)^2$: need both $\frac A{s-a}+\frac B{(s-a)^2}$. Irreducible quadratic: $\frac{As+B}{s^2+bs+c}$.
  • Complete the square, then split numerator to match $s-a$ and $\omega$: $\dfrac{s+2}{s^2+2s+10}=\dfrac{(s+1)+1}{(s+1)^2+9}\to e^{-t}\!\left(\cos3t+\tfrac13\sin3t\right)$.
  • Factor $e^{-as}$ out first. Invert the rest to $f(t)$, then answer $f(t-a)H(t-a)$.

The 4-step IVP method

  1. Transform every term, using the ICs in $\mathcal L\{y'\},\mathcal L\{y''\}$
  2. Solve algebraically for $Y(s)$
  3. Decompose (partial fractions / complete the square)
  4. Invert. Check $y(0)$, $y'(0)$
Non-unit leading coefficient: $4y''$ gives $4(s^2Y-sy_0-y_0')$. The ICs terms are multiplied too. E.g. $4s^2+12s+9=(2s+3)^2$: repeated root, gives $e^{-3t/2}$ and $te^{-3t/2}$.

Two-point conditions ($y(0)=0$, $y(t_1)=0$): keep $y'(0)=c$ as a symbol, invert, then solve for $c$ from the later condition. Pick the branch of the piecewise answer that applies at $t_1$.

Heaviside and t-shift

$$\mathcal L\{f(t-a)H(t-a)\}=e^{-as}F(s)\qquad H_a-H_b=\begin{cases}1&a

Key trap: $g(t)H(t-a)$ is not shifted until you rewrite $g(t)=g((t-a)+a)$: $$\mathcal L\{g(t)H(t-a)\}=e^{-as}\,\mathcal L\{g(t+a)\}.$$ E.g. $t\,H(t-2)$: $g(t+2)=t+2\to e^{-2s}\!\left(\frac1{s^2}+\frac2s\right)$.

Piecewise $\to$ Heaviside: add one term at each break: $f=f_1+(f_2-f_1)H_{a}+(f_3-f_2)H_b$. Heaviside $\to$ piecewise: evaluate each interval. The final answer to an IVP: piecewise, with $y$ and $y'$ continuous at the switch-on.

Dirac delta

  • $\int_{-\infty}^\infty f(t)\delta(t-a)\,dt=f(a)$ (sifting). $\mathcal L\{\delta(t-a)\}=e^{-as}$ ($a\ge0$).
  • Limit of narrow tall spike with area 1. $\delta$ is the derivative of $H$.
  • Impulse rule: $my''+\dots=k\,\delta(t-a)$: $y$ continuous, $y'$ jumps by $k/m$ at $t=a$. Always check both sides of the jump.
  • Impulse train $\sum\delta(t-n a)$: $\frac1{1-e^{-as}}$-type geometric series in $e^{-as}$.
  • Sketch: mark intercepts, the kink at the impulse, and the long-term behaviour.

Convolution

$$(f*g)(t)=\int_0^tf(\tau)g(t-\tau)\,d\tau,\qquad \mathcal L\{f*g\}=F\,G$$
  • $f*g=g*f$; $\ f*\delta_a=f(t-a)H(t-a)$; $\ 1*g=\int_0^tg$.
  • General forcing: $y''+py'+qy=g$, ICs zero: $Y=\frac{G}{s^2+ps+q}$, so $y=h*g$ with $h=\mathcal L^{-1}\!\left\{\frac1{s^2+ps+q}\right\}$ (impulse response). Non-zero ICs add the homogeneous part.
  • First order: $y'+ay=g$, $y(0)=y_0$: $y=y_0e^{-at}+e^{-at}*g$.
  • Integral eqn $y(t)=f(t)+\int_0^tk(t-\tau)y(\tau)d\tau$: $Y=F+KY\Rightarrow Y=\dfrac F{1-K}$. Integro-differential: transform, $\int_0^ty\to Y/s$.
  • Use convolution to invert a product $F\,G$ you cannot split.

Variable coefficients

$$\mathcal L\{ty'\}=-sY'-Y,\qquad \mathcal L\{ty''\}=-s^2Y'-2sY+y(0)$$

A first-degree-in-$t$ ODE becomes a first-order ODE in $Y(s)$: solve with an integrating factor. Fix the constant using $Y\to0$ as $s\to\infty$. $\ y''+2ty'+2y=0$ gives $-2sY'+s^2Y=sy_0+y_0'$.

Derivations to know

  • $\mathcal L\{f'\}$: integrate by parts, $e^{-st}f\big|_0^\infty=-f(0)$.
  • s-shift: $e^{at}$ merges into $e^{-(s-a)t}$.
  • t-shift: substitute $u=t-a$. $\ \ \mathcal L\{tf\}$: differentiate $\int e^{-st}f$ in $s$.
  • $F\to0$: $|F|\le\int_0^\infty e^{-st}Me^{ct}dt=\frac{M}{s-c}$.
  • Convolution: swap the order of integration over $0<\tau

Which tool?

  1. Constant coefficients, forcing $e^{at}$, trig, polynomial: standard IVP method
  2. Forcing switches on/off: Heaviside, t-shift
  3. Forcing is a hit at $t=a$: delta, jump in $y'$
  4. Forcing is a general $g$ (or an integral appears): convolution
  5. Coefficient has $t$: use $-F'$, solve first-order ODE in $Y$
  6. Coupled system: transform each equation, solve the $2\times2$ for $X(s),Y(s)$
