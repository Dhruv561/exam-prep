# Differential Equations (MAST20030) Resources

## Knowledge

- Lecture notes: `lectures/01–06_MAST20030_Lecture_Notes_Topic1_ODEsIVPs-LMS.pdf` (Thomas Quella, UniMelb, Sem 2 2026)
  The course's own notation and scope, so this is the top authority for what's on the exam. Use for: definitions, examples, which methods are examinable.
- Lecture notes Topic 2: `lectures/07–09_MAST20030_Lecture_Notes_Topic2_PowerSeries-LMS.pdf`
  Power series, ratio test, series method, Airy, Bessel order 0, uniform convergence, Weierstrass test, formal vs genuine.
- Paul's Notes, series pages: [Power series review](https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu/Classes/DE/PowerSeries.aspx), [Taylor series](https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu/Classes/DE/TaylorSeries.aspx), [Series solutions](https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu/Classes/DE/SeriesSolutions.aspx)
  Use for: extra worked index-shift and recurrence examples.
- [Paul's Online Math Notes: Differential Equations (Paul Dawkins, Lamar University)](https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu/Classes/DE/DE.aspx)
  Clear worked examples, a level above the lecture exercises. Use for: extra worked examples on integrating factors, 2nd-order constant coefficients, reduction of order, Wronskian, systems, phase portraits, Laplace, Fourier series.
  - [Definitions (order, linear, IVP)](https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu/Classes/DE/Definitions.aspx)
  - [Linear first-order equations (integrating factor)](https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu/Classes/DE/Linear.aspx)
- [MIT OCW 18.03SC Differential Equations (Fall 2011)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-03sc-differential-equations-fall-2011/)
  Full course with problem sets + solutions and short recitation videos. Use for: extra practice problems with worked solutions, especially systems/eigenvalues and Laplace.

- Problem booklets: `problem-booklet/MAST20030ProblemBooklet_Topic0–6.pdf` (answers at the back)
  Official practice at roughly exam level. Use for: homework after each lesson (assign by question number).
- Tutorial sheets + solutions: `tutes/tute_week_01–11(_sol).pdf`
  Week 1 = linear-algebra revision, weeks 2–3 = Topic 1, week 4+ = Topic 2 onwards. Use for: guided practice with full working.
- Past exams: `exams/` (2017, 2018, 2022, 2024, sample mid-sem). **Reserved for the user to sit cold: don't use in lessons.**
