# Style target + exam facts

## Style (from Modernia = Stats 2pp, Crown = LSM 4pp)
- Portrait, 3-4 vertical columns, black-ruled boxes filling the whole page, about 6.5-7pt. Word: Aptos body + Cambria Math.
- Bold lead-in term then a plain sentence. Plain English, no ornate wording.
- Crown: yellow highlight on section lead terms. Magenta key formulas. Blue key words. Red warnings / NOTE.
- Tables: Scenario | Pivot | CI (Modernia p2). Conjugate table. MLE table. ANOVA tables.
- "Maid Mast Codes" R boxes: monospace; bullet what each output line means; green R code.
- Q/A interpretation sentences (Crown p3-4). "Hard subquestions" box. Proof mini panels.
- Always state df, distribution under H0, when rejected, and the interpretation sentence.

## Handwritten MAS sheet (Cheat sheet.pdf): ideas worth stealing
- Worked past-paper snippets per topic: 2020 Q3 incomplete ll, 2020 Q4 Gibbs/MH/CAVI, 2021 M-step, 2022 Q(θ,θ⁰) with known labels, odds ratio CI.
- "give estimate" box: Poisson/binomial/gamma/normal fitted mean from linear predictor.
- KL intuition: q avoids regions where p is small, concentrates near mode, underestimates variance.
- Complete vs incomplete log-lik definitions. Gamma as exp family worked.
- NOTE its canonical link for gamma is written 1/μ; course form is θ = -1/μ, R inverse link is 1/μ.
