Native bees were trapped at 30 urban sites. \(y\) is the number of bees caught, flowers is floral cover (%), and dist is the distance to bushland (km). (Simulated data.)

(a) State \(n\) and \(p\). Which sites have high leverage by the \(2p/n\) rule?
(b) For site 19, compute \(r_P\), \(r_D\) and \(r_{SD}\). Use the slide 18 approximation to check the printed rstudent value.
(c) Compute Cook's distance for site 30. Site 30 has the highest leverage, so why is its Cook's distance smaller than site 7's?
(d) Using the plots and the drop-one table, classify sites 7, 19 and 30 as outlier, high leverage only, or influential. Justify each in one sentence.
(e) What does the line giving 1.622108 compute? Together with the residual deviance, what does it suggest? If you refit with quasipoisson, what is site 19's studentised Pearson residual?
(f) What does the residuals-vs-\(\hat\eta\) panel tell you about the model's assumptions?

(a) · \(n\), \(p\), leverage rule

$$\begin{aligned} n&=29+1=30\\ p&=30-27=3\\ \frac{2p}n&=\frac{6}{30}=0.2 \end{aligned}$$

\(h_7=0.41\gt0.2\) and \(h_{30}=0.83\gt0.2\); \(h_{19}=0.05\) doesn't.

High leverage: sites 7 and 30

(b) · Site 19

Pearson residual:

$$\begin{aligned} r_P&=\frac{y-\hat\mu}{\sqrt{\hat\mu}}\\ &=\frac{13-4.3091}{\sqrt{4.3091}}\\ &=\frac{8.6909}{2.0758}\\ &=4.19 \end{aligned}$$

Deviance residual:

$$\begin{aligned} d&=2\Big[y\log\frac y{\hat\mu}-(y-\hat\mu)\Big]\\ &=2\Big[13\log\frac{13}{4.3091}-8.6909\Big]\\ &=2\big[14.3549-8.6909\big]\\ &=11.328\\ r_D&=+\sqrt{11.328}\quad(y\gt\hat\mu)\\ &=3.37 \end{aligned}$$

Studentised (\(\phi=1\)):

$$\begin{aligned} r_{SD}&=\frac{r_D}{\sqrt{1-h}}\\ &=\frac{3.3657}{\sqrt{1-0.0487}}\\ &=\frac{3.3657}{0.9754}\\ &=3.45 \end{aligned}$$

Slide 18 approximation for the jack-knife residual:

$$\begin{aligned} r_J&\approx\sqrt{r_D^2+\frac{h\,r_P^2}{1-h}}\\ &=\sqrt{11.328+\frac{0.0487(4.1867)^2}{0.9513}}\\ &=\sqrt{11.328+0.897}\\ &=\sqrt{12.225}\\ &=3.50\quad\checkmark\ \text{(R: 3.496)} \end{aligned}$$

\(r_P=4.19\), \(r_D=3.37\), \(r_{SD}=3.45\)

(c) · Cook's distance for site 30

$$\begin{aligned} r_P&=\frac{25-25.6367}{\sqrt{25.6367}}\\ &=\frac{-0.6367}{5.0633}\\ &=-0.1257\\ C_{30}&=\frac{r_P^2\,h}{p\,(1-h)^2}\\ &=\frac{(0.1257)^2(0.8297)}{3(0.1703)^2}\\ &=\frac{0.01312}{0.08701}\\ &=0.15 \end{aligned}$$

Site 7 for comparison:

$$\begin{aligned} r_P&=\frac{10-6.1286}{\sqrt{6.1286}}\\ &=\frac{3.8714}{2.4756}\\ &=1.564\\ C_7&=\frac{(1.564)^2(0.4128)}{3(0.5872)^2}\\ &=\frac{1.0095}{1.0344}\\ &=0.98 \end{aligned}$$

Cook's distance needs leverage and a residual. Site 30 lies almost exactly on the fitted surface (\(y=25\), \(\hat\mu=25.6\)), so its tiny residual offsets its huge leverage.

\(C_{30}=0.15\lt C_7=0.98\)

(d) · Classify the three sites

  • Site 19: outlier. Jack-knife residual 3.50 jumps clear in the half-normal plot, but leverage 0.05 is ordinary and dropping it changes the coefficients only modestly (flowers 0.0284 → 0.0305).
  • Site 30: high leverage only. \(h=0.83\) is largest but its residual is near zero, and dropping it moves little (dist −0.115 → −0.124).
  • Site 7: influential. Cook's 0.98 is clear of the rest, and dropping it changes the dist coefficient by about 50% (−0.115 → −0.176).

Check site 7's data and report the fit with and without it. Don't just delete it.

(e) · The dispersion line

It computes the Pearson estimate:

$$\begin{aligned} \hat\phi&=\frac{X^2}{n-p}=1.62 \end{aligned}$$

The deviance agrees: \(41.15\gt\chi^2_{27}(0.95)=40.11\). Both suggest mild overdispersion.

Quasi-Poisson studentised Pearson residual:

$$\begin{aligned} r_{SP}&=\frac{r_P}{\sqrt{\hat\phi(1-h)}}\\ &=\frac{4.1867}{\sqrt{1.6221(0.9513)}}\\ &=\frac{4.1867}{1.2422}\\ &=3.37 \end{aligned}$$

\(r_{SP}(19)=3.37\): still large, so site 19 stays an outlier

(f) · Residuals vs \(\hat\eta\)

Deviance residuals scatter evenly around 0 with no trend or curve, so the log-linear form looks adequate. The spread is roughly constant, consistent with the Poisson variance function. Site 19 stands out at the top, and site 30 sits far right on zero, matching its high leverage and small residual.
