Exhibit used

Exhibit BOesophageal cancer (binomial glm)

The esoph data (base R) come from a case-control study in Ille-et-Vilaine, France. Each of the 88 rows is one combination of age group, alcohol group and tobacco group, with ncases cancer cases and ncontrols controls (\(m_i\) = ncases + ncontrols ranges from 1 to 60; 12 rows have \(m_i=1\)). Here age is a score 1–6 (1 = 25–34, 2 = 35–44, …, 5 = 65–74, 6 = 75+), alc is a score 1–4 (1 = 0–39 g/day, 2 = 40–79, 3 = 80–119, 4 = 120+), and tob is a factor with baseline 0–9 g/day. (The factors were converted to unordered ones so that R uses ordinary dummy variables.)

Because cases and controls were sampled separately, \(p_i\) is the probability that a subject in this study with those characteristics is a case. Retrospective sampling only shifts the intercept of a logit model, so the odds ratios still estimate population odds ratios (Faraway §4.3, p. 73).

(a) Explain why m1 is nested in m3. Test m1 against m3: state \(H_0\), the statistic, its df, and your conclusion. [3]
(b) Find the maximised log-likelihood of m3 and of the saturated model, and check the second against m1. Which model does AIC prefer? [3]
(c) Under m3, estimate the odds ratio of being a case for 120+ g/day versus 80–119 g/day drinkers (same age and tobacco), with a 95% CI. Interpret it in one sentence. [3]
(d) The colleague writes: "82.34 < 97.35, so m3 fits well." Comment. (Under m3, 75 of the 88 rows have \(m_i\hat p_i<5\).) [1]
(e) m3p has the lower AIC. Should you switch to the probit link? Give two reasons. [2]

(a) · Nesting and the LRT [3]

m1 forces age onto a straight line in its score: group \(k\) gets \((k-1)\beta_{\text{age}}\) relative to 25–34. Same for alcohol. So m1 is m3 with linear constraints on the coefficients.

$$\begin{aligned} p_{m3}&=88-76=12\\ p_{m1}&=88-82=6\\ s&=12-6=6 \end{aligned}$$

\(H_0\): age and alcohol effects are linear in the scores (m1 adequate).

$$\begin{aligned} \text{LR}&=D_{m1}-D_{m3}\\ &=106.91-82.34\\ &=24.57\\ \text{df}&=6\\ \chi^2_6(0.95)&=12.59 \end{aligned}$$

\(24.57\gt12.59\), so reject \(H_0\).

Scores are too restrictive: prefer m3

The 6 constraints: 5 age dummies → 1 slope (4), 3 alcohol dummies → 1 slope (2).

(b) · Log-likelihoods and AIC [3]

$$\begin{aligned} \hat\ell_{m3}&=p-\frac{\text{AIC}}2\\ &=12-\frac{221.39}2\\ &=-98.70\\ \ell_{\text{sat}}&=\hat\ell_{m3}+\frac{D_{m3}}2\\ &=-98.70+\frac{82.337}2\\ &=-57.53 \end{aligned}$$

Check with m1:

$$\begin{aligned} \hat\ell_{m1}&=6-\frac{233.96}2=-110.98\\ \ell_{\text{sat}}&=-110.98+\frac{106.91}2=-57.53\quad\checkmark \end{aligned}$$

\(221.39\lt233.96\), so AIC also prefers m3.

\(\hat\ell_{m3}=-98.70\), \(\ell_{\text{sat}}=-57.53\), AIC prefers m3

(c) · Odds ratio between two non-baseline levels [3]

$$\begin{aligned} \log\widehat{\text{OR}}&=\hat\beta_{120+}-\hat\beta_{80\text{–}119}\\ &=3.6029-1.9807\\ &=1.6222\\ \widehat{\text{OR}}&=e^{1.6222}=5.06 \end{aligned}$$

The variance of a difference needs the covariance:

$$\begin{aligned} \operatorname{Var}&=0.14825+0.08109-2(0.04402)\\ &=0.14130\\ \text{se}&=\sqrt{0.14130}=0.3759\\ \text{CI}&=1.6222\pm1.96(0.3759)\\ &=1.6222\pm0.7368\\ &=(0.8854,\ 2.3590)\\ \text{exp}&\Rightarrow(2.42,\ 10.58) \end{aligned}$$

Holding age and tobacco fixed, the odds of being a case for 120+ g/day drinkers are estimated to be about 5 times those for 80–119 g/day drinkers (95% CI 2.42 to 10.58).

Case-control design: it's an odds ratio, so don't turn it into a probability of getting cancer.

(d) · The colleague's fit claim [1]

Not valid. \(D\approx\chi^2_{76}\) needs expected counts of about 5 or more, but 75 of the 88 rows have \(m_i\hat p_i\lt5\). The data are too sparse, so 82.34 vs 97.35 is no evidence about fit.

(e) · Probit? [2]

No.

  1. AIC differs by only \(221.39-219.62=1.77\); logit and probit are nearly identical away from the tails, so the data can't really separate them.
  2. This is a case-control study. Under the logit, retrospective sampling only shifts the intercept, so coefficients stay valid log odds ratios. Probit coefficients have no such protection or odds reading.

Keep the logit
