The four-step recipe · Lesson 1

$$\ell(\theta)=\sum_i\log f(x_i;\theta),\qquad \ell'(\hat\theta)=0,\qquad J(\theta)=-\ell''(\theta),\qquad \mathcal I(\theta)=\mathbb E\,J(\theta;X),\qquad \hat\theta\overset{d}{\approx}N\!\big(\theta,\mathcal I(\theta)^{-1}\big)$$
  1. Log-likelihood: drop θ-free terms into \(c\) and collect data sums
  2. MLE: solve the score equation, then check \(\ell''(\hat\theta)<0\)
  3. Information: if \(\ell''\) has data in it, replace sums by expectations (\(\sum X_i\to n\mathbb E X\)). If not, say "\(J=\mathcal I\)"
  4. State \(\hat\theta\approx N(\theta, 1/\mathcal I(\theta))\). Wald CI: \(\hat\theta\pm z\sqrt{1/\mathcal I(\hat\theta)}\), with \(z_{0.975}=1.96\)
Traps
The course's \(\mathcal I\) is whole-sample (it contains \(n\)), so don't divide by \(n\) again · work in the parameter the question names (precision, 1/rate, …) · the MLE transforms by invariance but the information doesn't

CIs for a probability · Lesson 1

Build the CI on the logit scale \(\eta\), then map both ends through \(p=e^\eta/(1+e^\eta)\). The result stays inside \([0,1]\) and isn't symmetric (BinReg I, slide 40).

Binomial regression (logit) · Lesson 2

\(Y_i\sim\text{Bin}(m_i,p_i)\), \(\text{logit}(p_i)=\eta_i=x_i^T\beta\). \(\ell(\beta)=c+\sum_i[y_i\eta_i-m_i\log(1+e^{\eta_i})]\). Score \(\sum_i(y_i-m_ip_i)x_i\). \(\mathcal I(\beta)=\mathcal J(\beta)=X^TWX\), \(W=\text{diag}(m_ip_i(1-p_i))\) (no \(y\) in the Hessian).

CI for p at \(x_0\): \(\hat\eta_0=x_0^T\hat\beta\); \(\text{se}=\sqrt{x_0^TVx_0}\) (one predictor: \(V_{00}+2x_0V_{01}+x_0^2V_{11}\)); CI \(=g^{-1}(\hat\eta_0\pm1.96\,\text{se})\). Never \(\hat p\pm1.96\,\text{se}(\hat p)\).

Odds ratio for \(\Delta x_j\): \(e^{\hat\beta_j\Delta}\), CI \(\exp(\Delta(\hat\beta_j\pm1.96\,\text{se}_j))\); % change \(=(OR-1)\times100\%\).

Inverse links: logit \(1/(1+e^{-\eta})\) · probit \(\Phi(\eta)\) · cloglog \(1-\exp(-e^\eta)\). Logit: \(p=\tfrac12\) at \(x=-\beta_0/\beta_1\), slope there \(\beta_1/4\).
