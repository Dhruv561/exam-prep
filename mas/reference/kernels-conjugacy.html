Kernels, completing the square and conjugate priors

The algebra under every Gibbs, MH and CAVI question, on one printed page. SHEET = on the exam's formula sheet, so leave it off your A4. A4 = not provided: copy it onto your notes. Taught in Lesson 15.

The master move

\(p(\theta\mid\text{rest})\propto p(\text{everything})\) as a function of \(\theta\): drop every factor with no \(\theta\), add exponents, match a kernel below. What counts as constant depends on the variable: \(\tau^{n/2}\) (from \(\prod\sqrt{\tau/2\pi}\)) is a constant for \(\mu\) but not for \(\tau\). Support: \((0,\infty)\) suggests Gamma/Inv-Gamma, \((0,1)\) Beta, \(\mathbb R\) Normal. Intro Bayes slide 8; MCMC slides 21–22

Completing the square A4

$$-\tfrac A2\mu^2+B\mu=-\tfrac A2\big(\mu-\tfrac BA\big)^2+\tfrac{B^2}{2A}$$

Expand, collect: \(\mu^2\) coefficient \(=-A/2\), \(\mu\) coefficient \(=B\). Then \(N(B/A,1/A)\). CAVI derivations p. 3

Normal–Normal (\(\tau\) known, \(\mu\sim N(m_0,1/\kappa_0)\)): \(A=\kappa_0+n\tau\), \(B=\kappa_0m_0+\tau\sum x_i\). Precisions add; the mean is the precision-weighted average of \(m_0\) and \(\bar x\). Slide form: prior \(N(\mu,\sigma^2/n_0)\Rightarrow N\big(\frac{n_0\mu+n\bar x}{n_0+n},\frac{\sigma^2}{n_0+n}\big)\). slide 11

Normalising-constant integrals A4

$$\int_0^\infty\tau^{a-1}e^{-b\tau}d\tau=\frac{\Gamma(a)}{b^a}\qquad\int_{\mathbb R}e^{-\frac A2\mu^2+B\mu}d\mu=\sqrt{\tfrac{2\pi}A}\,e^{\frac{B^2}{2A}}$$ $$\int_0^1\theta^{\alpha-1}(1-\theta)^{\beta-1}d\theta=B(\alpha,\beta)=\tfrac{\Gamma(\alpha)\Gamma(\beta)}{\Gamma(\alpha+\beta)}$$

Use for the evidence \(p(x)=\int p(x\mid\theta)p(\theta)d\theta\), e.g. Poisson–Gamma: \(p(y)=\frac{\Gamma(a+y)}{\Gamma(a)y!}\frac{b^a}{(b+1)^{a+y}}\) (neg. binomial). Beta–Binomial: \(\binom mx\frac{B(\alpha+x,\beta+m-x)}{B(\alpha,\beta)}\). \(p(x)\) has no \(\theta\), so it cancels in \(\propto\) and in MH ratios.

Where it's used A4

  • Gibbs: each full conditional = the joint, read in one parameter. If it isn't a known kernel, use an MH step (or a finite sum for a discrete parameter). MCMC slide 13
  • MH: \(A=\min\Big(1,\frac{\pi(\theta^*)q(\theta^*,\theta)}{\pi(\theta)q(\theta,\theta^*)}\Big)\). \(\pi\) is unnormalised (\(p(x)\) cancels). \(q\) cancels only if symmetric. Log-normal walk: \(q\) ratio \(=\theta^*/\theta\). Independence \(q=g(\cdot)\): ratio \(=g(\theta)/g(\theta^*)\). slides 5, 8, 10
  • CAVI: \(\log q_j^*=\mathbb E_{-j}[\log p(x,\theta)]+\)const. Same kernel as Gibbs, with \((x_i-\mu)^2\to(x_i-\mathbb E\mu)^2+\operatorname{Var}\mu\) and \(\tau\to\mathbb E\tau=a^*/b^*\). VI slides 19–22

R traps (reading code)

  • rgamma(n, shape, rate): the 3rd positional argument is the rate; scale = 1/rate. Lecturer: rgamma(1, a1 + cfreq[M], b1 + M) = Gamma(shape, rate). coal p. 2
  • rnorm(n, mean, sd): sd, not variance. So the full conditional \(N(B/A,1/A)\) is rnorm(1, B/A, sqrt(1/A)).
  • 1/rgamma(1, a, b) = one draw from Inv-Gamma(\(a,b\)).
  • exp(logpost(prop) - logpost(cur)) = \(\pi(\theta^*)/\pi(\theta)\), computed on the log scale. MH example p. 6
Traps
Dropping \(\tau^{n/2}\) when the target is \(\tau\) · reading the power as the shape (shape = power + 1) · Exponential–Gamma: \(n\) goes to the shape and \(\sum x\) to the rate · Inv-Gamma mean is \(b/(a-1)\), not \(b/a\) · forgetting the proposal ratio in MH · the rate vs scale mix-up in rgamma · \(\operatorname{Var}\mu\) goes in the CAVI rate, never the shape.
